We Bring the Salon to Your Door!

Say goodbye to stressful car rides and long waits at the grooming salon! Shiny Paws offers convenient mobile dog grooming services, bringing professional pampering right to your doorstep. Our fully equipped vans and experienced groomers ensure your dog receives the same high-quality care as they would in a traditional salon, all in the comfort and familiarity of their own home.

Benefits of Shiny Paws Mobile Dog Grooming

  • Stress-Free Experience: No more anxious car rides or waiting in unfamiliar surroundings. Your dog can relax and enjoy their grooming session at home.
  • Personalized Attention: Our groomers provide one-on-one care, ensuring your dog's individual needs and preferences are met.
  • Convenient Scheduling: We offer flexible appointment times to fit your busy schedule.
  • Comprehensive Services: From haircuts and baths to nail trims and ear cleaning, we offer a full range of grooming services.
  • Serving the Treasure Coast: We proudly bring our mobile salon to dog owners throughout the Treasure Coast, including Port St. Lucie, Stuart, Vero Beach, Fort Pierce, and Sebastian.

Ready to give your pet the star treatment? Book your haircut appointment online or call us at 772-777-5568.
