Expert Anal Gland Expression for Your Dog's Comfort

Anal gland expression is an important part of your dog's hygiene routine. At Shiny Paws, we provide safe and gentle anal gland expression services to help your furry friend stay comfortable and healthy. Why is Anal Gland Expression Important?

  • Prevents Discomfort and Complications: Impacted or full anal glands can cause your dog discomfort, scooting, and even infections.
  • Promotes Hygiene: Regular expression helps maintain good hygiene and prevents unpleasant odors.
