Untangle Your Dog's Coat with Expert Dematting

Matted fur can be painful and stressful for your dog. At Shiny Paws, our experienced groomers provide gentle and effective dematting services to restore your furry friend's comfort and confidence. We use specialized tools and techniques to carefully remove mats and tangles without causing any discomfort.

Our Dematting Services include:

  • Thorough assessment of the severity of matting
  • Gentle removal of mats using specialized tools and techniques
  • Soothing bath and conditioning treatment to restore coat health
  • Professional brushing and styling to prevent future matting

Why Choose Shiny Paws for Dematting?

  • Experienced & Caring Groomers: Our team is passionate about dogs and trained in the latest dematting techniques.
  • Pain-Free Approach: We prioritize your dog's comfort and well-being throughout the dematting process.
  • Preventative Care: We offer tips and advice on how to prevent future matting and keep your dog's coat healthy.
  • Convenient Mobile Service: We proudly serve dog owners throughout the Treasure Coast, including Port St. Lucie, Stuart, Vero Beach, Fort Pierce, and Sebastian.
