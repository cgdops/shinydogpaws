Where Your Dog's Comfort Comes First

At Shiny Paws, we understand that bath time can be stressful for some dogs. That's why we've created a calming and stress-free environment where your furry friend can relax and enjoy their pampering session.

Our Dog Bath Services include:

  • Warm, soothing hydro massage bath
  • Gentle, hypoallergenic shampoos and conditioners
  • Thorough ear cleaning and nail trimming
  • Luxurious blow-dry and brushing

Why Choose Shiny Paws for Your Dog's Bath?

Our team is passionate about dogs and trained in the latest grooming techniques. We tailor each bath to your dog's individual needs and preferences. Our mobile salon is meticulously cleaned and disinfected after each appointment.
